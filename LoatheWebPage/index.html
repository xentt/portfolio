About

Loathe are a genre-defying force from Liverpool, England. Blending the crushing weight of metalcore with the ethereal textures of shoegaze and the rhythmic pulse of nu metal, they create soundscapes that are simultaneously punishing and beautiful.

Formed in 2014, the band quickly gained attention for their uncompromising vision—a fusion of visceral screamed vocals and haunting clean melodies layered over baritone guitar riffs and industrial-tinged electronics.

Their breakthrough album I Let It in and It Took Everything (2020) was hailed as a landmark record, earning a place among Metal Hammer's best metal albums of the year and drawing comparisons to Deftones and Radiohead.
